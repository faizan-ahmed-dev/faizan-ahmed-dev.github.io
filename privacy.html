Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This site (faizanahmed.me) is a personal portfolio. It doesn't sell anything, doesn't run ads, and doesn't have user accounts. Here's exactly what data touches this site and where it goes.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand traffic - roughly how many people visit, which pages and projects get explored, and what device/browser they're using. Google Analytics collects standard technical data such as:

  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not exact)
  • Device type, browser, and screen size
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referral source (how you arrived here)

No personally identifying information is intentionally collected or sold. You can read Google's own policy at policies.google.com/privacy, and opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all sites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Contact form

The contact section at Farpoint Lighthouse doesn't submit anything to a server. When you send a message, it opens your own email client with a pre-filled draft addressed to mehunfaizan@gmail.com - the message only ever leaves your device once you personally hit send in your own mail app.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set first-party cookies to distinguish sessions and visitors. No other cookies are set by this site.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at mehunfaizan@gmail.com.
