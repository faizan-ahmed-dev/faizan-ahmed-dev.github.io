Faizan Ahmed - "Ricocheted Ball"

I'm Faizan Ahmed, a Computer Science student (3.66 GPA) who likes turning strange ideas into things people actually want to use. My favorite proof of that: 404: Dino Not Found, a game I built from concept to finish under Pakistan Game Jam 2026's constraints — it went on to win the jam by public vote. Beyond games, I've shipped several full-stack software projects, from desktop apps to database-backed systems, each teaching me something the last one didn't.

I'm currently seeking internships, collaborations, and opportunities to work on ambitious projects with people who are passionate about creating exceptional technology.
